Our Story Since 1939

Since 1939, Riff's has been part of the fabric of Newfoundland and Labrador life. Family-owned, community-first, and proudly local.

1939: The Beginning

A small general store opened in Windsor, Newfoundland and began a legacy built on trust and value.

1970s to 1990s: Growth

As communities grew, Riff's opened new stores from coast to coast across the province.

Today: 20 Stores Strong

Still 100% family-owned. Still committed to quality, value, and the people of NL.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for shoppers looking for Riff's stores, departments, and local information.

When is the Riff's Black Friday sale in 2026?

Riff's Black Friday Sale runs from Monday, November 16 to Sunday, November 22, 2026, with a new 50% off deal each day and 33 1/3% off almost everything else in the store. See this week's daily deals.

Where can I shop the Riff's Black Friday sale in Newfoundland and Labrador?

Shop Black Friday at Riff's stores across Newfoundland and Labrador, including Grand Falls-Windsor, Gander, Clarenville, Deer Lake, Marystown, Bonavista, Lewisporte, Springdale, St. Anthony, Port aux Basques and Happy Valley-Goose Bay. Call your local Riff's to confirm hours and availability.

What are Riff's Black Friday hours?

Riff's is open late on Thursday, November 19 and Friday, November 20, and open 1 PM to 5 PM on Sunday, November 22. Hours vary by location, so call your local Riff's to confirm.

Does Riff's offer layaway during the Black Friday sale?

Yes. Riff's offers a free layaway plan. Ask at your local Riff's store for details.

How many Riff's Department Store locations are there?

What does Riff's sell?
